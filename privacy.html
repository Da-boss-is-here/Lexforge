Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

LexForge is a vocabulary trainer. This policy describes what data the app handles and how. The short version: LexForge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. LexForge does not collect any personal information about you.

No account is required. No email address, name, or identifier is requested. No analytics or crash reporting is enabled.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in LexForge — your word bank, practice history, error log, and settings — is stored locally:

  • On iOS: in the app's own Documents directory on your device. It is included in your device backups if iCloud Backup or Finder backup is enabled.
  • In the web version: in your browser's local storage, on your device only.

Your data never leaves your device. LexForge makes no network requests and has no server.

Third parties

LexForge does not share any data with third parties, because no data leaves your device.

The app includes two open-source libraries — Chart.js and ts-fsrs — that run entirely on-device. Neither transmits data anywhere.

Data you can export

The app includes Export and Import features in Settings. When you export, a JSON file is created on your device and handled by your operating system's file or share dialog. You control where that file goes.

Children's privacy

LexForge is safe for users of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, no special provisions are needed for users under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the project's GitHub repository: github.com/Da-boss-is-here/Lexforge/issues
